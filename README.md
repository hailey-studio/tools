# tools

Two small free tools for students applying abroad. Plain HTML, no build step, no sign-up, nothing leaves the browser.

| Tool | What it does | Link |
|---|---|---|
| 留学吞金兽 | Total cost of a degree abroad (UK, US, HK, SG, AU, CA) in RMB, months to pay it back, and a shareable receipt image. Every number links to the school or visa page it came from. | [hailey-studio.github.io/tools/tunjinshou](https://hailey-studio.github.io/tools/tunjinshou/) |
| 死线航空 | Works back from your deadlines to when to sit IELTS/TOEFL/GRE, ask for references and finish essays. Exports to your calendar (.ics) and makes a boarding-pass image. | [hailey-studio.github.io/tools/sixian](https://hailey-studio.github.io/tools/sixian/) |

Fees and timings were checked in November 2026. Always confirm with the school's own site.
